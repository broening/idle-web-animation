# Code of Conduct

## Our aim

This project should be a place where anyone can take part without being
harassed. That holds in issues, pull requests, commits, and any other space
the project uses.

## Expected

- Be respectful of other people and other viewpoints.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and help newcomers where you can.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this, report it to
**mail@website-spezialist.de**. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer
may remove comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that break these rules,
and may block repeat offenders from the project.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in
public.
